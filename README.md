# FiveGen — AI Growth Operator

FiveGen builds the launch around a creator’s expertise. The creator supplies their expertise and recordings; AI helps plan the work, draft written product content and launch materials, and prepare an authentic video sales letter (VSL). The creator reviews the work before publishing. A published landing page captures real leads in the creator’s CRM.

## Current product

One **Create your product** action opens the creator workflow. Workspace navigation is **Chat with Gen, Dashboard, Products, Customers, Creator library, Calendar**. Each selected product has **Overview, Content, Sales page, Price & access, Marketing, Customers and Settings**. Overview explains the next action; a separate **Launch review** destination owns required-content checks, editorial approval and delivery publication. **Price & access** owns commercial terms, payment setup, service availability and the explicit sales switch. Missing requirements have direct repair actions.

**Chat with Gen** supports ordinary conversation and starting from a rough idea. For creation requests, Gen proposes an ordered plan with a sketch, the reason for each step, required inputs, and the exact draft to review. Separate approval buttons save written product drafts in **Products**, campaign drafts in **Marketing → Campaigns**, and internal events in **Calendar**. Only the next step can be approved; typing approval in a message never executes a step. The conversation remains saved, saved-work links survive revised plans, and the product editor’s **Chat** button returns to the conversation. Execution uses the reviewed content without another AI generation charge. Planning uses the existing message allowance and displayed credit ceiling. External publishing, sending and recording remain separate actions. Chat keeps its input docked while the transcript scrolls. Active follow-up questions appear one at a time in the input area; answers and an optional note are sent together. View plan includes a materials ZIP with actual proposed drafts, an SVG workflow sketch and calendar imports when relevant; assistant replies can also be downloaded individually. Downloads are proposal snapshots and do not execute approvals.

New mini/video courses open directly in **Curriculum** with **Upload recording, Write a lesson or Add a resource**. Original teaching stays private until deliberately reviewed and published. Saved product audience, Outcome, tone and inclusions provide defaults for one shared marketing brief; saved shared answers are overrides. Existing marketing drafts and approved copy are preserved when that brief changes. An unset paid price reads **Price not set** in sales-page previews and public rendering; **Free** requires an explicitly free offer or tier.

Marketing has four task groups: **Plan** (Growth plan, Funnel Builder, offer research and creator references), **Create** (Sales video, Lead capture and Campaigns), **Follow up** (Emails), and **Results**. Optional shortcuts still respect saved visibility preferences. The sales page owns the public offer; Lead capture owns separate signup/application pages. Emails foregrounds the sequence and moves sender, page and activation setup into one checklist. Results starts with the recorded journey and an explained next action; share-link detail and business earnings are expandable. Gen is docked in the workspace header rather than covering editor controls. See the [creator clarity release checklist](docs/creator-clarity-release-checklist.md) for the seven approved changes and their verification boundaries.

1. **Checkout and customer access.** Stripe Connect Accounts v2 provides full-dashboard creator accounts that receive direct charges. Checkout supports free, one-time, recurring, pay-what-you-want and configured bundle offers in USD, EUR, GBP, CAD and AUD. Draft and published pricing remain separate; the exact published billing interval is shown before purchase. Signed webhooks verify account, mode, amount, currency, email, and payment state before granting access. Buyers get a private portal with purchased lessons, recordings, and files. A purchase cookie lasts 30 days; signing in with the matching verified email recovers access. Consulting purchases include intake, one session booking, creator availability, cancellation/rebooking, and a private meeting link. Refunds (including partial refunds) and disputes revoke access; disputes do not automatically restore access when closed. New checkouts collect a FiveGen application fee based on the creator’s verified paid plan: Free 5%, Creator Pro 3%, Scale 0%. The earnings dashboard shows actual Stripe fees, FiveGen fees and creator net by settlement currency; pending fees remain unknown, and historical checkouts keep their original fee policy. Test and live records are separated.
2. **Email automations.** Creators edit and approve welcome/nurture/launch sequences and purchase onboarding. Approved snapshots are immutable for enrolled recipients; draft edits require reapproval for future enrollments. Marketing requires explicit consent, includes unsubscribe, and stops after a verified live purchase. Purchase onboarding requires active paid access. A durable SQLite queue uses leases, bounded retries, and Resend idempotency. Pausing stops pending sends. Delivery logs distinguish queued, paused, accepted by provider, failed and suppressed states; provider acceptance is not inbox delivery. Application receipts, actual booking/reschedule and cancellation notices use separately approved transactional templates for future events. They grant no marketing permission or access. Test purchases never trigger customer email.
3. **Creator Voice Library.** Store owner-wide stories, video/audio recordings, transcripts, and creator-attested evidence with permission controls. Optional OpenAI transcription uses the existing server key, costs the displayed 10 credits (or included unlimited access), accepts at most 25 MB, and requires review. Up to 100 sources; 20,000 characters per transcript. Only approved sources enter AI context. VSL uses the five latest approved sources; campaigns select up to eight. Context is bounded to 6,000 characters per source and 18,000 overall. Voice recordings remain creator-only, even if mistakenly referenced by public content. Removing a library entry retains the uploaded recording until explicitly removed.
4. **Campaign Studio.** Generate or manually write social posts, short-video scripts, and launch emails using the offer brief and selected source citations. Edit and approve drafts, choose each item’s **Planning account**, review its explicit reminder time zone, set a publishing reminder, copy/export text, and export an `.ics` calendar. **Open shared Calendar** opens the account-wide Calendar filtered to that campaign and product; there is no second campaign calendar. 5. **Offer Lab.** Compare two customer/outcome/package/price hypotheses, share a public preference survey at `/o/<slug>`, collect real feedback, and copy a chosen offer into the VSL brief. Publishing is an explicit snapshot; changed offers start a separate research round. Survey participation does not opt people into marketing or charge them. Results are directional audience feedback, not a prediction of sales.
6. **Growth overview.** Track anonymous daily visits, VSL plays, CTA clicks, new leads, bookings, paid orders, and revenue. Product and campaign-tag views use actual saved events. Test/live payments and currencies stay separate; revoked orders and purchases of FiveGen credits are excluded. Totals describe period activity rather than a single visitor cohort. Rule-based next actions explain their evidence. Event collection respects Do Not Track and does not store email or IP addresses.

The existing VSL interview covers customer, problem, beliefs, story, promise, proof, offer, tone, and CTA. AI creates editable growth copy using administrator-managed OpenAI/Anthropic credentials, with 10/30-credit generation, idempotency, concurrency guards, and refunds on failure. Product creation keeps course content creator-authored. Manual modules, lessons, private recordings, resources, generated creative assets, CRM notes/status/export, AI credits, historical billing, and assistant connections remain available. ## Useful product and launch guidance

After a newly approved product draft saves, Chat with Gen shows an animated completion dialog and opens that exact product after five visible seconds. Open product continues immediately; Stay in chat, Escape or dismissal cancels the handoff. The countdown pauses in hidden tabs. History loads, manual tasks, skipped actions and failed saves never trigger it. Draft creation does not publish or enable sales.

The teaching entry starts a focused mini course with original teaching; other formats remain available. Product details is the shared home for the buyer problem and prerequisites used by generation. Legacy prerequisites remain visible until deliberately replaced or cleared.

Lesson objectives and action steps are edited beside the teaching, and unlocked buyer lessons show the saved objective before the content. Practice advice recognizes existing action steps, assignments, quizzes and reflections. A recording alone does not claim an activity was inspected; advice stays optional and recording-only lessons need no invented transcript.

Launch guidance separates reviewed delivery publication, public Sales page publication and enabling buyer access. Creators can prepare the page before payment setup, and dashboard resume links open the actual next task. Existing review, exact-version publication and checkout eligibility checks remain authoritative.

## Plans and credits

Every account receives 3 complete AI product runs and 20 planning messages per UTC month. Manual editing and downloads are free. Additional content costs 10 credits per OpenAI step or 30 per Anthropic step. Images cost 12 credits and 5-second videos with audio cost 160.

Current monthly subscriptions are Creator Pro at $29/month with 2,500 credits and a 3% product-sale fee, and Scale at $99/month with 6,000 credits and a 0% product-sale fee. Free has a 5% sale fee. Stripe processing is additional on every tier. Legacy Pro annual and Studio subscriptions retain their original billing and credits and qualify for the 3% rate while eligible; they are not offered for new signups. Subscription credits expire monthly; one-time purchased credits do not expire. The existing packs remain 1,000/$15, 2,500/$35 and 6,000/$75. Annual plans never release twelve months of credits upfront. See [studio release notes](docs/creation-studio.md) and [sandbox catalog IDs](docs/paddle-subscriptions-sandbox.json).

## Run and configure

Use Node 24.13+. Run `npm ci`, `npm run build:render`, then `npm run start:render` with the existing private configuration in `.env.render.local`. Production uses Render with a persistent SQLite/media disk; see [Render setup](docs/render.md). The optional Sites/Cloudflare target remains available through `npm run dev` and `npm run build`.

Additive migrations `0011_growth_operator.sql` and `0012_creator_growth_suite.sql` introduce the growth and creator-business tables; later numbered migrations extend them without replacing records. The Render runtime applies pending migrations on startup; the Cloudflare target must receive the same migrations. No historical migration or billing record is replaced.

Only administrators supply shared AI credentials. Never commit env files or expose server API keys to the client. A cloned repository does not include the production database or its encrypted credentials: local live AI requires the administrator’s existing configuration. The local fixture preview does not fabricate AI output or call providers.

## Stripe Connect setup

Use the ignored `.env.render.local` file (local) or private deployment environment (production). Never paste secret keys into the browser, client code, a commit, or a public issue.

- `STRIPE_MODE=test` and `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`: start with a matching test key. The existing Stripe AI-wallet integration uses the same mode/key but a separate webhook.
- `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`: signing secret for **connected-account snapshot events** sent to `https://YOUR_DOMAIN/api/commerce/webhook`.
- Subscribe to `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`, `charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created`, `charge.dispute.closed`, and `account.updated`.
- `STRIPE_COMMERCE_ENABLED=true` only after the connected-account webhook is configured. Missing setup leaves settings editable but connect/checkout unavailable.
- Each creator opens Account menu → Seller payments, supplies their actual business country, and completes Stripe-hosted onboarding once for all products. They can publish before connecting payments; they finish content, set a price and explicitly enable sales when ready.
- Customer portal: `/customer`. For development, Stripe CLI forwarding can deliver Connect events to `/api/commerce/webhook`; use the signing secret printed by that CLI session, never a placeholder. See [Accounts v2 configuration](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/accounts-v2/connected-account-configuration), [Stripe Connect direct charges](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges) and [Connect webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/webhooks).

Live credentials can be kept in the ignored `.env.stripe-live.local` profile while `.env.render.local` remains in test mode. Load that profile explicitly only for a production runtime with a production database and the live connected-account webhook. The `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY_LIVE` name is a private staging value; the runtime reads `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` from the selected profile. The prepared live profile leaves commerce, wallet checkout, and the email worker disabled. Restricted keys need Accounts v2 and Account Links v2 write access, plus connected-account Checkout write and Payment Intent/Charge read access. Read-only verification does not prove write permissions. See [Stripe restricted-key permissions](https://docs.stripe.com/keys/restricted-api-keys).

Product sales activation is scoped to the selected Stripe mode. Enabling a test offer never enables it in live mode; enable and save the product again after live onboarding. Local fixture previews reject live Stripe keys.

For a local Connect preview, install the official Stripe CLI and run `npm run preview:connect` (or set `STRIPE_CLI_BIN` to its executable). This test-only helper forwards connected-account events and starts the isolated studio preview; its signing secret stays in memory. Keep it running during testing. It does not modify deployment configuration.

The supplied local Stripe test key has been authenticated. A real test creator account was created through Accounts v2; no charges or account agreements were submitted. End-to-end real Stripe fulfillment still requires a connected account, webhook delivery, and a test payment. Live enablement, account agreements, tax configuration, and deployment require the account owner's review.

## Email delivery and runtime

Configure `RESEND_API_KEY` and a verified `EMAIL_FROM` address (no display name) through existing administrator settings or server environment, plus canonical `APP_ORIGIN`. Creators supply their sender name, reply-to address, and postal address in Automations. Set `AUTOMATION_DISPATCH_SECRET` to a random secret of at least 32 characters. The Render server calls its authenticated internal dispatch route once per minute, with at most five jobs per batch. Durable records catch up after restarts; retries stop before Resend's 24-hour idempotency-key retention expires.

`AUTOMATIONS_WORKER_ENABLED=false` disables the Render timer. The local preview always disables it. An external scheduler may POST `/api/automations/dispatch` with `Authorization: Bearer <AUTOMATION_DISPATCH_SECRET>`; configure one for a Cloudflare deployment because the Node timer runs only on Render. See [Resend idempotency](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/idempotency-keys). No email is sent merely by saving a sequence. The creator must explicitly review and activate it. No live marketing messages were sent during development.
