# Products, delivery, funnels, and partners

FiveGen has dedicated builders for 11 product types: books/eBooks, guides, playbooks, mini courses, full/video courses, template kits, challenges, consulting/mentorship, coaching sessions, communities, and custom products. Each follows the same creation pipeline while collecting the fields and content appropriate to its type. Users can work with AI, upload existing content, or start manually. Originality, content accuracy, and commercial success are not guaranteed; creators review their work before publishing or selling it.

## Creator workflow

1. Choose the product type and starting point. AI uses the administrator's shared content/image/video providers; customers do not supply API keys. Manual drafts start empty, and uploading existing content does not spend AI credits.
2. Build the product in its dedicated editor. Add its essential details, type-specific fields, sections, original files and optional cover. The right-side AI assistant can be minimized. AI edits are reviewed before applying; saved editorial revisions support undo/history. Moving to the next step or opening account payment settings saves first.
3. Review delivery and readiness. Empty sections, missing original files, unfinished placeholders and other required content checks block publication. Courses can include private MP4/WebM recordings, up to 100 MB per upload; split longer recordings into lessons. This is authenticated private file streaming with range requests, not transcoding or adaptive streaming. Creating a course, including recordings, or offering a session does not require a Pro feature unlock.
4. Publish the product page. Payment setup is optional for publication, and a funnel is not required. Configure Stripe once in Account → Seller payments; every product reuses that account connection. New paid checkout stays unavailable until the platform configuration, seller onboarding, published price and product delivery are ready.
5. Start selling and share the published product page. Verified payment grants access to the purchased content snapshot. The customer can return in the checkout browser using its private HttpOnly purchase cookie, or sign in with the same verified email used at checkout. A `/customer?order=…` URL selects a purchase; the order ID or email link alone grants no access.

All accounts can use the creation tools. The standard free allowance is three complete AI-generated products per UTC month; further AI work and image/video generation use credits according to the displayed cost. Manual products are not capped at ten. Subscriptions and one-time credit packs fund usage; they do not unlock the product builders or video/session delivery tools.

For new seller checkouts, FiveGen's fee is **5% on Free, 3% on Creator Pro, and 0% on Scale**, plus Stripe's separate processing fees. A reduced rate requires a verified paid plan entitlement for the sale's payment environment. Eligible earlier Pro/Studio subscriptions retain the 3% rate while their paid period remains valid. The server freezes each checkout's fee when it is created; upgrades, cancellations and later policy changes never alter an earlier sale's recorded rate. Spending included AI credits does not change the seller fee, and buying a credit pack does not change the creator plan.

## Customer library and delivery by type

The private customer library presents the purchased title, description, appropriate product-type labels, and a cover where the purchased snapshot includes an eligible image. Covers use an authenticated order-specific route; a buyer never receives the creator's current private draft cover, source media or raw upload URL. Historical purchases without cover metadata use a designed fallback.

- Books, guides and playbooks provide an organized reader with chapters, sections or process steps and downloadable resources.
- Mini/video courses provide a lesson selector, private inline recordings, next/previous navigation and completion controls. Challenges use the same flow with self-paced days, daily-time guidance and completion criteria. Selecting a lesson/day and marking it complete saves progress **in that browser's local storage for that order**; this is not cross-device/server-synced completion. If browser storage is unavailable, the content still opens and the interface explains that progress cannot be saved.
- Template kits show usage instructions, compatible-tool notes and individual original/editable files. Custom products follow their selected supported delivery family.
- Consulting/coaching purchases show the purchased scope, preparation and available session times. One paid modern purchase includes one bookable session; customers can cancel a confirmed booking and choose another available time. The private meeting link appears after a booking is confirmed.
- Community purchases show the welcome, guidelines, shared resources and the creator's private external community invitation link. The current library does not create a native discussion feed or a new external community automatically.

Original purchased content remains available when new sales are paused. Published PDF updates are included for existing buyers, while new modules require the creator's explicit grant. Refunds, including partial refunds, and disputes revoke modern paid access; disputed access is not restored automatically. Checking a pending checkout cannot hide or revoke the customer's other paid products. Legacy customer ZIPs continue to exclude launch campaigns and marketing prompts; resource filenames remain unchanged so packaged references keep working.

## Marketing and sales

Each product's Marketing section opens its sales dashboard by default. Creator voice, VSL, funnel, campaigns, automations and offer testing are optional plugins that the owner adds explicitly; opening a deep link does not install a plugin. CRM remains a separate workspace section. A book or uploaded product can be published and sold without adding any marketing tool.

AI-produced scripts/campaign copy are drafts, not recorded videos or sent messages. Publishing a funnel or activating an email sequence requires its own readiness/approval checks. The sales dashboard distinguishes verified sales, FiveGen fees, Stripe fees and actual net earnings where provider accounting is available; pending accounting and different settlement currencies stay explicit. Earnings do not guarantee an immediately available bank payout.

## Retained legacy funnels and booking

The earlier storefront/partner routes are retired in the production boundary. Their historical records and access adapters remain for existing purchases and support; the details below describe that retained system, not a Pro-only requirement for the current builders.

Retain historical private-link access where the legacy adapter already uses it; never copy those bearer tokens into new confirmation emails or current library links. Legacy classroom completion remains stored against stable lesson IDs, and historical subscription purchases retain their original server-side subscription access checks. Taking an offer off sale does not erase a purchase, its content revision, progress or grants.

Retained funnel data supports five starting layouts: sales, VSL, free guide, booking, and post-booking. Presets and edits preserve section order, copy, colors, typography, layout, CTA and supported media. Historical VSL publication has its own video and entitlement guards; do not bypass them or expose an unpublished creator recording through the current library.

A free-guide funnel can deliver the same free product or another published free product owned by the creator. Names and emails become CRM leads. Marketing consent is separate, optional, and initially unchecked; providing an email for access does not grant marketing permission.

The legacy booking engine allows customers to reserve creator-supplied times after acquiring a booking product. Each paid purchase or paid renewal provides one session; a free product provides one introductory session. A future appointment can be cancelled and rebooked. A completed or missed session uses its allowance; a new paid purchase provides another. Only one upcoming appointment can be held at a time. Overlapping appointments across the creator's products are rejected. Calendar downloads, preparation instructions, meeting links and an optional preparation video appear in its confirmation. Attendance is recorded by the creator after the scheduled start. These legacy allowances are separate from the current one-session-per-order service flow.

Legacy CRM reports retain captured leads, bookings, recorded attendance and gross paid sales. Booking rate is captured product leads with a non-cancelled booking divided by captured leads in the selected period. Show-up rate is attended divided by attended plus no-show; future/unmarked appointments are excluded. Its sales figures are gross, before refunds, platform fees, partner shares and Stripe processing. Legacy tables/CSV contain up to 1,000 records; headline metrics cover the full selected period. Do not substitute these gross reports for the current verified net-earnings ledger.

## Retained referral agreement and payout safeguards

Historical partner agreements record a 1–80% commission (stored to hundredths of a percent), acceptance using the invited verified email, and an eligible connected Stripe account for payouts. The retired invitation, partner and payout routes are not part of the current creation pipeline. Preserve the following rules when reviewing or servicing retained agreements:

- The agreed percentage applies to this product's discounted paid price, excluding add-ons. Only the customer's first paid purchase of the product qualifies; subscription renewals and self-referrals do not.
- Accepted partners get a unique `/r/…` URL. The last visited partner link for that product is remembered for 30 days in an HttpOnly cookie. It is browser-specific, not cross-device attribution.
- Invitations expire after 14 days. A pending invitation can be replaced from Partners; its previous link stops working. To change an accepted percentage, revoke the agreement and send a new one. Revoking stops future attribution and does not erase earned commissions.
- FiveGen's normal platform fee and the reserved partner share are collected together through a Stripe Connect application fee. The first subscription payment reserves the partner share; subsequent invoices use only FiveGen's current plan commission.
- Eligible earnings can be claimed after a 14-day hold. A claim transfers funds from the platform balance to the partner's Stripe account; Stripe controls subsequent bank payout timing. Transfers require a supported account/country and sufficient platform balance.
- A refund, including a partial refund, or a dispute cancels the entire referral commission. Previously transferred shares are reversed before returning the reserved application-fee portion to the seller. Stripe can reject reversals if funds are unavailable; failed events must be retried and reconciled. Keep a platform reserve for these obligations.
- Claims and reversals use Stripe idempotency and persisted state. An uncertain transfer is searched by its commission group before a retry. Unresolved attempts beyond 20 hours require administrator reconciliation; never issue an extra manual payout without checking Stripe first.

The agreed percentage is a sales commission, not ownership or equity in a product. A reserved partner share is additional to the historical fee captured for its checkout. Do not apply the current Free 5% / Creator Pro 3% / Scale 0% policy retroactively to a retained agreement, rewrite past fees, or recreate payouts to match a new plan. AI/media credits are separate from both seller fees and partner commissions.

## Resend configuration

The super administrator connects Resend in Platform settings → Provider connections and enters a verified sender under Email & availability. Alternatively set `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM` on the server. Secrets are encrypted when stored in the admin connection and never returned to customers. The read-only Purchase confirmations section shows configuration readiness and queued/sent/failed counts; Refresh never sends or resends mail. See [Resend sender documentation](https://resend.com/docs/api-reference/emails/send-email).

Without Resend, current product access and booking details remain available in the authenticated customer library, while new purchase confirmations stay queued. The app does not claim an email was sent. Retained invitation/legacy booking handlers use Resend with their original idempotency keys when explicitly available; this connection alone never activates marketing campaigns.

Verified **new live product purchases** also create a durable transactional confirmation in `purchase_emails` (migration `0023_purchase_emails.sql`). The paid transition and queue insertion are atomic. No message is sent during checkout fulfillment, and existing purchases are never backfilled when deploying. Sandbox purchases create a `suppressed` record and never send real email. Legacy file offers remain sandbox-only; their audit records are always suppressed because the retained legacy order schema has no authoritative refund/revocation state. A legacy row can never authorize a live confirmation. Free downloads and renewal invoices do not generate duplicate purchase confirmations.

The branded HTML and plain-text confirmation names the purchased item and includes its private access page and an optional purchase-email sign-in link. Links use canonical `APP_ORIGIN`, never a request Host header. Emails contain no bearer tokens, private file addresses, manuscript content, or unpublished cover URLs. Opening a link still requires the original purchase cookie or authentication with the checkout email.

Automatic delivery requires Resend, its verified sender, and canonical HTTPS `APP_ORIGIN`. The production process runs a dedicated purchase email dispatcher once per minute, independent of `AUTOMATIONS_WORKER_ENABLED` and all marketing sequences. It generates a random server-only loopback credential in memory at startup; no secret setup is needed. Set `PURCHASE_EMAILS_WORKER_ENABLED=false` to pause these confirmations. The private `POST /api/purchase-emails/dispatch` endpoint accepts only that dispatcher credential, never a customer/admin cookie alone. Missing configuration leaves mail pending without starting a retry clock. The administrator can inspect readiness, counts, and recent delivery states at `GET /api/admin/purchase-emails`; this endpoint requires an administrator session and does not expose message bodies or recipient addresses.

Concurrent workers claim leases and retry the same frozen payload and Resend idempotency key after transient errors. `sent` records provider acceptance, not proof of inbox delivery. Unconfirmed sends stop after six attempts or 23 hours from the first attempt, before Resend’s 24-hour idempotency window expires. Review the saved provider identity in Resend before contacting a buyer after an ambiguous failure; do not manually send an extra confirmation or delete the delivery record. Revoked modern purchases are canceled before sending. The email queue never changes buyer entitlements.

## Deployment and checks

Render applies append-only migrations at startup. Historical customer journey records begin with `0005_loose_madripoor.sql`; current builders, retained PDF updates, revisions, creator fee plans and purchase emails use the later additive migrations, including `0017`–`0023`. Keep the existing database, disk, credential encryption key, payment records and permanent provider infrastructure.

The current **connected-account sales** destination at `/api/commerce/webhook` handles `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`, `charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created`, `charge.dispute.closed` and `account.updated`. The platform subscription destination keeps its separate billing events. Retained legacy subscription handlers use their original `invoice.created`/`invoice.paid` events; do not remove or repurpose permanent destinations to consolidate the two flows. See [Stripe direct charges and application fees](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges?platform=web&ui=elements).

After `npm run build:render`, run `npm run test:buyer-experience`, the Render integration checks, and the affected commerce/credit suites. Keep the retained journey/referral regression checks for changes to those adapters. Buyer tests cover type-specific presentation, browser-local progress, authenticated media and private purchase confirmations. Financial tests use synthetic identities/provider transport and isolated SQLite fixtures; email tests mock Resend and send no messages. These checks do not establish successful live payment, inbox delivery, provider content quality or bank payout.
