## New interfaces and routes

- `CreatorContextPicker`: `productId`, optional `disabled`, `onChange(workspace)`, `onDirtyChange`, `onBusyChange`. Existing `/api/builder/:id/context` remains the source of truth.
- `ConnectedLaunch`: persisted launch selection and state-based journey/review queue on Marketing’s Launch tab.
- `GET/POST /api/products/:id/connected-launch`: preview/current facts; selecting a recipe saves only goal and tool visibility.
- `GET /api/products/:id/marketing-activity`: owner-only actual activity summary used before hiding tools.
- `GET/POST /api/products/:id/marketing-versions`: owner-only inspect/restore campaign or VSL/page draft versions, revision-guarded. Restored campaign items are drafts with no reminders.
- `GET/POST /api/products/:id/campaigns/import-emails`: no-save import preview and explicit approved-copy append into the lead sequence draft.
- `GET /api/products/:id/offer-lab/brief-preview?variant=a|b`: previous/proposed brief fields and exact revision for apply review.
- `POST /api/products/:id/page-copy`: generation of a separate saved proposal; `action:validate` rechecks sources, saved page and brief before local apply.
- `PageCopySuggestion`: `productId`, `pageUpdatedAt`, `current:{headline,heroDescription}`, `aiReady`, `cost`, `unlimited`, optional `disabled`, `onBusyChange`, and `onApply(copy,proposal)`. It does not save/publish the page.

## Approved marketing-role consolidation: MR-10, MR-13 and MR-19 campaign identity

Implemented locally on 2026-10-25. These changes are code and local verification, not a claim of a production deployment or successful live email delivery.

- **MR-19 campaign identities:** migration `0052_campaign_email_destinations.sql` copies each legacy singleton campaign into `marketing_campaigns` with the stable default ID equal to its product ID. The old table is retained as migration backup. New campaigns receive separate UUIDs, content/revisions, source provenance, destination, AI lease, version history and named links. Selected campaign is stored per owned product and a `campaign` query parameter resolves a specific campaign. The Campaigns selector uses the shared Save & continue registry before switching; failed saves retain the current copy. Campaign creation is bounded at 100 per product.
- **MR-10 preparation to delivery:** each reviewed campaign email can append a new delivery draft or explicitly update its linked draft step. The saved step contains its campaign ID, item ID, exact revision, content hash, reviewed references, brief revision and destination. The same item cannot be appended twice; updates retain step identity. The handoff shows current draft/approved status and a bounded count of recent provider-accepted jobs. A searchable link table is only an index; embedded source-to-step identity inside the optimistic sequence write is authoritative for dedupe and remains correct after a crash between the write and audit indexing. Campaign copy approval remains separate from email activation.
- **MR-13 reviewed destinations:** new lead drafts default to the published canonical Sales page when available. Owners can choose another published sales, lead or application page, and each imported campaign email can keep its own explicit destination. UI previews show the real resolved path/version. Approval re-resolves every effective destination and atomically checks its publication identity. Future enrollment and delivery recheck that the reviewed publication remains available; a changed publication requires explicit review for future delivery. An approved sequence snapshots its destination URL; already enrolled snapshots and retry identity/payload are never rewritten. Legacy enrolled snapshots without destination metadata retain compatibility behavior. `product_url` is the preferred template token; `funnel_url` remains supported and maps to the explicitly reviewed destination for new snapshots.
- **Tracked links and Results integration:** new named links validate the real owned campaign and its saved revision server-side, derive its title and selected page reference, and store campaign/page identities and publication metadata. Redirects resolve the selected page's current published version, rather than freezing a checkout price or granting access. Campaign Results can filter by the stable campaign ID through the unique named link code. Historical links remain compatible. Foundation owns the shared scoped Results UI/API.
- **Personal replies and Customers:** Emails now has an independently saved personal reply template with optimistic revision checks and a copy action. Until explicitly saved, the API offers the previous VSL follow-up as legacy copy; later VSL generation cannot replace a separately saved reply template. This path never sends or enrolls anyone. The existing CRM can consume `/api/products/:id/reply-template`. Email activity accepts owner-resolved opaque customer references, filters recent recipient timelines and offers a validated local return-to-person link. Provider acceptance is labeled separately from inbox delivery.
- **Consent control styling:** the Emails text-field reset now excludes checkbox/radio/file/color controls so the activation checkbox does not inherit text-input padding. General marketing checkbox sizing is handled by the foundation workstream.

### Final shared-page AI and mutation-race verification

`scripts/page-copy-isolation-test.mjs` now runs the actual shared-page services, authoritative facts helper and proposal/CAS engine against synthetic SQLite records. Two additional pages deliberately share the same numeric revision: generation and validation retain their separate page IDs, never save either page or the canonical page, and cannot reuse one request ID across pages. Editing one page leaves the sibling suggestion valid, while editing that suggestion's page, saved product facts or builder version blocks apply. Mid-generation page/product changes fail the final guard and refund the mocked reservation; old proposals missing fact review cannot be silently backfilled. API checks retain page identity, owner isolation and origin protection. All generation output is mocked.

`scripts/public-page-races-test.mjs` independently verifies the final conditional writes: a competing draft edit during recording validation cannot publish old copy; a recording becoming private after byte inspection fails publication; unpublish after public resolution but before media authorization blocks playback; unpublish or replacement publication before the lead insert blocks both lead/consent storage and receipts/enrollment; a changed saved product after snapshotting blocks publication. These tests pass against the actual services and storage adapters with no provider operations. An already authorized response/stream is not claimed to be retroactively retractable.

The page-copy isolation suite also exercises the actual `PageCopySuggestion` client handlers through the real API: two mounted page clients transmit their own `pageId`, apply only their own reviewed local callback, leave every stored page unchanged, and refuse the callback when a competing page edit makes the proposal stale.

## Creator clarity follow-up — 2026-10-25

This follow-up keeps the existing tool responsibilities and verification boundaries while simplifying how creators reach them. Marketing now groups work into Plan, Create, Follow up and Results. Saved tool visibility remains authoritative; changing groups or hiding a shortcut does not discard work or pause execution.

The product brief is shared rather than repeated across tools. Blank audience, Outcome, tone and inclusions can inherit saved Product details, and a nonblank shared answer remains an override. Outcome never defaults to the product description. The compact reference links to the common editor and back to the originating tool; changing the brief preserves existing campaign, page, VSL and approved email copy. Generation still needs the current reviewed context and separate approval.

Campaign items now retain Planning account and reminder time zone. Campaigns opens the global Calendar with campaign/product scope; assigned reminders appear under their owned planning profile, and unassigned work remains a general reminder. There is one calendar UI, with source edits staying in Campaigns. No social authorization, automatic publication, email send or confirmed booking is implied by scheduling.

Emails starts with the sequence journey and copy, with sender/page/activation setup in one disclosure. Results starts with recorded journey evidence and an explained next action; share-link detail and business earnings remain available in disclosures. These presentation changes do not remove consent, exact-version approval, mode/currency distinctions, provider-acceptance caveats or event-scope limitations.
